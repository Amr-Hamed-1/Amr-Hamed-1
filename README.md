Computer Science graduate (Mansoura University, July 2026) and AI / ML engineer focused on **Generative AI, Advanced RAG, and NLP**. I build projects end to end: data preprocessing, model fine-tuning, quantitative evaluation, and containerized deployment with FastAPI and Docker.

I started in data analytics (Python, SQL, Power BI, Excel) and moved into ML and LLM engineering, so I care about measuring results, not just getting a demo to run.

- 🎙️ Graduation project: **MindSense AI**, a multimodal psychological-state analyzer (grade: Excellent). I owned the voice component.

- 🎯 Interested in **NLP and Arabic audio processing**.
